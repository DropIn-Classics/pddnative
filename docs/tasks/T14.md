# T14: the FLI animations and the quality of life fixes (Claude, on master, 2026-09-28)

## What was made

- `port/src/fli.c`, `fli.h`: the DELUXE folder's .FLI files played as
  DDFLIPLY.EXE plays them (docs/tasks/T9.md): COLOR_64, LC, BLACK, BRUN
  and COPY decoded; the header's frame count shown (not the ring frame),
  speed x 41h units of a 4660.9 Hz clock between two frames; a key ends
  the playback (a key as kbhit sees one: not the shifts, Ctrl, Alt or the
  lock keys) and the key buffer is emptied. `fli_before_table` does what
  DDPCMAIN's `run_selection` does around DDFLIPLY: the last picture held
  46h retraces, then `L0EE5` (the DAC read back and faded to black in 32
  steps of one retrace: `L10D9`, factor 31/32 down to 0/32). Read from
  DDPCMAIN's code; DDFLIPLY sets mode 13h (its one INT 10h, AX=0013h) and
  does not set a text mode before its exit, so its last picture is what
  stays on the screen (read from its bytes, not run).
- The launcher: "Quality of life fixes" on the main page, `qol` in
  pdd.cfg, on by default. Off: `fli_before_table` before the table. On:
  `pd_qol` for the engine (`main.c`); `-qol` gives it to a table started
  with `-prog`/`-table`.
- The engine (`pd_qol`, `qol_frames`, pd.h): the waits listed in
  port/README.md ("The setup screen"); `ball_start`'s two long waits end
  with the jingle before them (`wait_jingle`, pd_play.c), at most as long
  as the original's. `jingle_playing` (pd_sound.c): set when a jingle
  (2 and up) starts, cleared by the next pattern jump (`module_callback`)
  and whenever the tune takes over.

## Measured

A headless build with an audio capture (a scratch copy of plat_null.c
that pulls sound.c's samples at each picture; not committed), F1, the
plunger, one ball drained without the flippers, on all eight tables:

| | original's waits | with the fixes |
|---|---|---|
| F1 to the ball in the lane (pictures) | 429 on all | 259-384 |
| silent pictures in it | 0-186 | 0-40 |
| ball lost to the next ball in the lane | 717-765 | 371-517 |
| silent pictures in it | 259-407 | 19-85 |

What is left silent: the table scrolling down after the ball (17
pictures), and the empty rows at the end of a jingle's pattern before the
jump that ends it (20-45 pictures). On Revenge of the Robot Warriors the
ball-lost jingle and jingle 3 last longer than the original's waits:
there the waits' caps end them, as in the original.

A jingle's end is its first pattern jump: seen in PD_TRACE runs of all
eight tables (the jump notes of the scratch build), not read from the
modules; a jingle with a jump inside it would end the wait early.

## Checked

- The FLI decoding: all twelve files through `fli.c` with a scratch
  platform against `tools/flifiles.py`'s decoding, every picture and
  palette equal; the playing time the header's frames x speed x 41h /
  4660.9 Hz (8.41 s for a table's, 5.86 to 16.18 s for the intro's).
- The flow with a scratch platform that says it has a window: `qol = 0`
  in pdd.cfg, F2 on the launcher: Steel Wheel's animation, then the table;
  Space during the animation ends it, the hold and the fade follow, the
  table does not get the key; `qol = 1`: the table at once.
- `portcmp.py --table 1 idle_loop#3000`: equal (the headless build does
  not set `pd_qol`). A whole game (portplay seed 1, Steel Wheel: 9,570
  frames of play, the roulette, hold_bonus, level switches) compared at
  st_ball_lost#1, st_ball_start#2, st_ball_lost#2, #3 and st_game_over#1:
  all equal.
- `-Wall -Wextra -Wconversion -Wshadow -pedantic`: no warnings in the
  files changed.
